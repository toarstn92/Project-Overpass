From the Valued Living — ACT Self-Help Manual (Ford): “This part of you is there through it all and it doesn’t change. This part of you doesn’t get affected by the thoughts. It doesn’t challenge them or try to change things; it simply observes as they happen.” The manual also reminds us, “Therefore, although you have thoughts, you must be more than your thoughts to be able to notice them.”
